# AItoB Domain Glossary

AItoB connects local LLMs, internal enterprise tools (Gmail, Finance CSV/XLSX, RAG), and external customer communication channels (LINE Official Account) through Open WebUI.

## Core Language

**Case**:
A customer communication lifecycle initiated from LINE, tracked as a dedicated chat session in Open WebUI until archived.
_Avoid_: Ticket, issue, session, inquiry

**Customer**:
An external person communicating through the LINE Official Account.
_Avoid_: User, client, caller, contact

**Admin**:
An internal authenticated operator in Open WebUI who reviews and sends messages.
_Avoid_: Agent, moderator, support

**AI Draft**:
A preliminary suggested reply generated by the local assistant model placed into the admin input area for review.
_Avoid_: Auto-reply, template, suggestion

**Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)**:
The operational policy requiring an admin to inspect and explicitly dispatch any external communication to a customer.
_Avoid_: Manual check, approval flow
